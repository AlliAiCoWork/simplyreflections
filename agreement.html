2Background and Purpose

Simply Reflections provides professional window cleaning, power washing, solar panel cleaning, bird guard installation, garbage can cleaning, junk removal, and window and patio screen services. From time to time the Company engages independent subcontractors to perform that work on a job-by-job basis. In performing the work, the Subcontractor will be given access to the Company’s customers, pricing, business methods, and other confidential information, and may be given the temporary use of Company equipment.

This Agreement sets out the terms under which the Subcontractor performs work, protects the Company’s legitimate business interests, and confirms that the Subcontractor operates as an independent business and not as an employee.

Appendix A is part of this Agreement. The checklist attached as Appendix A — Subcontractor Requirements and Responsibilities lists the Subcontractor’s specific duties and acknowledgments in plain language. Appendix A is incorporated into and made a part of this Agreement, and must be initialed and signed together with it.

Appendices B and C are part of this Agreement. Appendix B — Scope of Services and Performance Standards describes each service the Company may assign to the Subcontractor and the standard to which it must be performed. Appendix C — Screen Measurement and Installation Schedule applies only when the Company assigns the Subcontractor screen measurement or installation work. Both are incorporated into and made a part of this Agreement.

3Independent Contractor Status

3.1 Separate business. The Subcontractor represents and warrants that he, she, or it is engaged in an independent trade, occupation, or business, holds itself out to the public as such, and is free to perform services for others. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employer-employee, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship.

3.2 No employee status or benefits. The Subcontractor is not an employee of Simply Reflections and is not eligible for, and will not receive, employee benefits of any kind, including health insurance, paid time off, sick pay, holiday pay, overtime, retirement contributions, or unemployment benefits.

3.3 No Form W-2. The Subcontractor will not receive a Form W-2 from the Company. No federal, state, Social Security, Medicare, or other tax will be withheld from any payment made under this Agreement.

3.4 Form 1099. Where required by law, the Company will issue the Subcontractor a Form 1099-NEC at the end of the calendar year reporting the total compensation paid.

3.5 No workers’ compensation. The Subcontractor is not covered by the Company’s workers’ compensation insurance and waives any claim to such coverage. Obtaining workers’ compensation or occupational accident coverage for the Subcontractor and for any of the Subcontractor’s own workers is solely the Subcontractor’s responsibility, to the extent required by law.

3.6 Control of the work. The Subcontractor controls the manner, method, and means by which the work is performed, supplies its own labor, and sets its own hours, subject only to the customer’s scheduled service window and the requirements of this Agreement. The Company specifies the result to be achieved, not the manner of achieving it.

3.7 Guidance is not training. The Subcontractor may ask the Company for insight, information, or guidance about the requirements of a specific job or a customer’s expectations. Any such insight is provided as an accommodation only. It does not constitute training, is not offered or paid for as training, is not compensable time, and shall not be construed as evidence of control or of an employment relationship. Simply Reflections does not offer, provide, or pay for training of any kind.

3.8 Unique, non-routine job sites. Each job assigned under this Agreement is a separate and unique engagement at a distinct job site. Work is offered job by job. It is not part of a regular schedule, route, or ongoing rotation for the Subcontractor and creates no expectation of continued or future work. The Subcontractor is free to accept or decline any job offered. This Agreement does not guarantee any minimum amount of work or compensation.

3.9 The Subcontractor’s own workers. If the Subcontractor uses employees, helpers, or lower-tier subcontractors, they are the Subcontractor’s sole responsibility — including hiring, supervision, payment, taxes, insurance, workers’ compensation, and safety. The Subcontractor shall ensure that each such person complies with this Agreement.

4Taxes and Required Documentation

4.1 Documents required before work begins. Before starting any job, the Subcontractor must complete and deliver to the Company all required federal, state, and local tax documents — at a minimum, a current IRS Form W-9, together with any state tax registration, licensing, or exemption forms applicable in Arizona or California. The Company may withhold the assignment of work and the payment of invoices until those documents are received.

4.2 Sole responsibility for taxes. The Subcontractor is solely responsible for reporting and paying all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, and any other taxes or fees arising from payments under this Agreement, and shall indemnify the Company against any resulting liability, penalty, or interest.

4.3 Licensing. The Subcontractor shall obtain and maintain any business license, registration, or contractor licensing required in the jurisdictions where the work is performed.

5Insurance

5.1 Subcontractor carries its own insurance. The Subcontractor is required to obtain and maintain, at its own expense and for the entire period of engagement, its own insurance — including, at a minimum, commercial general liability insurance in a commercially reasonable amount; workers’ compensation coverage where the Subcontractor has workers, as required by law; and valid automobile liability insurance for any vehicle used in connection with the work.

5.2 Proof of coverage. On request, the Subcontractor shall furnish a current certificate of insurance and, where requested, shall name Simply Reflections LLC as an additional insured.

5.3 No coverage by the Company. The Subcontractor is not insured by Simply Reflections and shall not represent to any customer or third party that it is.

6Safety and OSHA Compliance

6.1 Own safety. The Subcontractor is solely responsible for its own safety and the safety of its workers, and for supplying and correctly using appropriate personal protective equipment, ladders, lifts, fall protection, and other equipment.

6.2 Regulatory compliance. Compliance with federal OSHA requirements and all applicable state, county, and municipal safety regulations — including the Arizona Division of Occupational Safety and Health (ADOSH) and California Division of Occupational Safety and Health (Cal/OSHA) — is the Subcontractor’s sole responsibility, for itself and for its own employees.

6.3 Job site inspection. The Subcontractor shall inspect each job site for hazards before beginning work and shall decline or stop work that cannot be performed safely. The Company does not direct or supervise the Subcontractor’s safety practices.

6.4 Assumption of risk. The Subcontractor assumes the risk of injury to itself and its workers and releases the Company from liability arising from the Subcontractor’s own acts, omissions, or failure to follow safety requirements, except to the extent caused by the Company’s gross negligence or willful misconduct.

7Company Equipment — Lease, Care, and Return

7.1 Equipment is leased, not transferred. Any tools, ladders, poles, water-fed systems, pumps, hoses, vehicles, or other equipment provided by the Company are leased to the Subcontractor for the limited term of a specific job (the “Lease Term”) and remain the sole property of Simply Reflections LLC at all times. No ownership interest passes to the Subcontractor.

7.2 Full liability for leased equipment. The Subcontractor is fully liable for all equipment leased or otherwise entrusted to the Subcontractor, from the moment it is received until it is returned to and accepted by the Company. This liability includes loss, theft, damage, destruction, and damage caused by misuse, neglect, or improper storage or transport. The Subcontractor shall pay the Company the cost of repair or the full cost of replacement, at the Company’s election.

7.3 Maintenance. The Subcontractor shall maintain all leased equipment in clean, safe, working condition — cleaning it after use, storing and transporting it securely, and reporting any defect, malfunction, or damage to the Company immediately.

7.4 Twenty-four hour return requirement. All leased equipment must be returned to the location designated by the Company within twenty-four (24) hours of the completion of the work for which it was issued, unless the Company grants an extension in writing.

7.5 Notice if the return will be late. If the Subcontractor cannot return equipment within the twenty-four (24) hour window, the Subcontractor must notify the Company in writing before that window closes, stating the reason and the date and time the equipment will be returned. Equipment that is not returned on time and has not been the subject of such a notification is “overdue and unreported.”

7.6 Escalating liability after seventy-two (72) hours without notification. The Parties acknowledge that Company equipment is placed in service on scheduled jobs, and that equipment which is missing and unaccounted for causes the Company losses well beyond the item’s own value — including income lost on jobs that cannot be performed, completed, or must be rescheduled; the cost of renting or purchasing replacement equipment on short notice; harm to customer relationships and to the Company’s reputation; and the disruption and distress caused to the Company’s business operations. Accordingly, if equipment remains overdue and unreported for seventy-two (72) hours or more past the time it was due to be returned, the Subcontractor’s liability is not limited to the replacement value of the equipment and may amount to a multiple of that value. In that circumstance the Subcontractor shall be liable to the Company for the greater of:

  • The full replacement cost of the equipment, plus the Company’s documented lost income, rental or emergency replacement costs, and other losses caused by the absence of the equipment; or
  • Liquidated damages equal to three (3) times the replacement value of the equipment.

The Parties agree that the Company’s actual losses in this situation are difficult to calculate precisely at the time of signing, that the amount above is a reasonable estimate of those losses and not a penalty, and that this remedy is in addition to — not in place of — the Company’s other rights under this Agreement. Timely written notification under Section 7.5 does not excuse the return obligation or the Subcontractor’s liability for the equipment itself; it prevents only the escalation described in this Section 7.6.

7.7 Written permission. For purposes of this Section, “in writing” includes a text message (SMS) or an email from or to an authorized representative of Simply Reflections. Verbal permission or verbal notice is not sufficient.

7.8 Retention beyond the Lease Term. Retaining, withholding, selling, pawning, loaning, or otherwise failing to return Company equipment after the Lease Term or after a demand for its return may constitute theft, embezzlement, or unlawful conversion of the property of another under applicable Arizona or California law. The Subcontractor acknowledges and understands that the Company may report such conduct to law enforcement and that the Subcontractor may be criminally prosecuted, in addition to any civil remedy the Company may pursue.

7.9 Right of offset. The Company may deduct the cost of unreturned, lost, or damaged equipment, and any amount owed under Section 7.6, from any amounts owed to the Subcontractor.

8Professional Conduct on Job Sites

8.1 Professionalism required. The Subcontractor shall conduct itself in a professional, courteous, and respectful manner at all times while on or near a job site, while in view of a customer, and whenever identifiable as performing work for Simply Reflections.

8.2 No arguments or confrontations. The Subcontractor shall not engage in arguments, verbal altercations, threats, harassment, intimidation, or physical confrontations with:

  • Homeowners, tenants, occupants, property managers, or other representatives of the job site;
  • Other subcontractors, or the employees or workers of another subcontractor;
  • Employees, agents, or representatives of Simply Reflections; or
  • Any general contractor, vendor, or other trade present at the job site.

Disagreements or disputes are to be reported to the Company for resolution rather than addressed on site.

8.3 Standards of conduct. The Subcontractor shall:

  • Maintain a clean, presentable appearance and wear appropriate work attire;
  • Refrain from profanity, offensive language, and discriminatory or harassing conduct;
  • Not be under the influence of, or consume, alcohol, cannabis, or illegal drugs on a job site;
  • Respect the customer’s privacy, property, pets, and household;
  • Not photograph, record, or post a customer’s property or job site on social media or elsewhere without prior written consent; and
  • Arrive on time and notify the Company promptly if delayed or unable to complete a job.

8.4 Consequence. Conduct that violates this Section is a material breach of this Agreement and may result in immediate removal from the job site, termination of the engagement, and offset against amounts owed, in addition to any other remedy available to the Company.

9Customer Property and Damage

9.1 Duty of care. The Subcontractor shall exercise reasonable care to protect the customer’s real and personal property, including glass, window frames, screens, sills, doors, landscaping, flooring, furnishings, fixtures, vehicles, and structures.

9.2 Damage is a violation. Damage to the real or personal property of a customer caused by the Subcontractor, its workers, or its equipment is a violation of this Agreement.

9.3 Deduction from monies owed. The Company may deduct the cost of repair, replacement, remediation, customer credit, or refund arising from such damage from any monies agreed upon under the estimate for the job, from the invoiced amount for the work performed, or from any other amounts payable to the Subcontractor then or later. If the cost exceeds the amounts owed, the Subcontractor shall pay the balance to the Company on demand.

9.4 Reporting. The Subcontractor shall report any damage to the Company immediately and shall not negotiate, settle, or promise compensation to a customer without the Company’s prior written approval.

9.5 Indemnity. The Subcontractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Company from and against any claim, loss, damage, or expense (including reasonable attorneys’ fees) arising from the Subcontractor’s work, acts, omissions, workers, or equipment.

10Invoicing and Payment

10.1 The Subcontractor invoices the Company. For each job completed, the Subcontractor shall submit an invoice to Simply Reflections for the monies owed for the work performed. Payment is made against a properly submitted invoice.

10.2 Invoice contents. Each invoice shall include, at a minimum:

  • Invoice date and invoice number;
  • Job site address and customer name;
  • Date or dates of service and a description of the work performed;
  • The agreed price or agreed rate for the job; and
  • The Subcontractor’s business name, tax identification number, and remittance information.

10.3 The Subcontractor’s own accounting. It is solely the Subcontractor’s responsibility to prepare and submit invoices and to manage its own accounts payable and receivable, either directly or by engaging a third-party bookkeeping or invoicing company at the Subcontractor’s own expense. The Company does not maintain payroll, bookkeeping, or accounting for the Subcontractor.

10.4 Agreed amounts and change orders. Payment is limited to the agreed estimate or agreed price for the job. Additional work or change orders must be approved by the Company in writing (including SMS or email) before the work is performed. Unapproved additional work is not payable.

10.5 Offsets. All payments are subject to the deductions and offsets permitted by this Agreement, including those in Sections 7 and 9.

11Non-Competition

11.1 Restriction Period. For a period of sixty (60) months (five years) following the end of the Subcontractor’s engagement with the Company (the “Restriction Period”), the Subcontractor shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or perform window cleaning or substantially similar services for any customer or client that the Subcontractor serviced, or was introduced to, through Simply Reflections.

11.2 Arizona applicability. For work performed in the State of Arizona, this provision is governed by and construed in accordance with Arizona Revised Statutes (A.R.S.) § 23-1501 et seq. and applicable case law. The Parties agree that the scope, duration, and geographic limitations are reasonable and no greater than necessary to protect the Company’s legitimate business interests, including its confidential customer relationships and proprietary methods.

11.3 California applicability — important notice. Under California Business & Professions Code § 16600, non-compete agreements are generally void and unenforceable in the State of California. To the extent any work is performed in California, the non-competition restriction in Section 11.1 shall not be enforced. The non-solicitation and non-disclosure provisions in Sections 12 and 13 remain in full force and effect in California to the extent permitted by applicable law.

12Non-Solicitation

During the Restriction Period, the Subcontractor shall not, directly or indirectly:

  • Solicit, contact, or attempt to divert any customer or account of the Company that the Subcontractor became aware of through the engagement; or
  • Encourage any Company employee, contractor, or vendor to leave or reduce their relationship with the Company.

13Non-Disclosure of Confidential Information

13.1 Definition. “Confidential Information” includes, but is not limited to: customer names, addresses, and contact information; pricing structures and service rates; cleaning methods, products, and techniques used by the Company; scheduling and route information; and any other non-public information relating to the Company’s business operations.

13.2 Obligation of confidentiality. The Subcontractor agrees to (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose it to any third party without the Company’s prior written consent; and (c) use it solely for the purpose of performing services under this Agreement. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

13.3 Exceptions. The confidentiality obligation does not apply to information that:

  • Is or becomes publicly known through no act of the Subcontractor;
  • Was rightfully known to the Subcontractor before the engagement; or
  • Is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided the Subcontractor gives the Company prompt written notice of the requirement.

14Consideration

In exchange for signing this Agreement, the Company agrees to engage the Subcontractor for window cleaning services and to provide access to its established customer base, materials, and work opportunities. The Subcontractor acknowledges that this constitutes adequate and sufficient consideration. For the avoidance of doubt, the consideration described here does not include training, which the Company does not provide.

15Remedies for Breach

The Subcontractor acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement would cause the Company irreparable harm that cannot be fully remedied by monetary damages alone. In addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity, the Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief to prevent or stop a breach, to offset amounts owed as provided in this Agreement, and to terminate the engagement immediately. In any successful enforcement action, the prevailing party may be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys’ fees and costs.

16Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either Party. Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by written notice (including SMS or email). Termination does not affect (a) the Subcontractor’s obligation to return equipment, (b) amounts owed by either Party, or (c) Sections 3, 7, 9, 11, 12, 13, 15, and 17, which survive termination.

17Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Arizona for work performed in Arizona, and by the laws of the State of California for work performed in California, without regard to conflict-of-law principles. Any dispute shall be resolved in the state or federal courts of the applicable state. To the extent any provision is unenforceable in Arizona or California, it shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, or severed, without affecting the remaining provisions.

18Severability and Blue Penciling

If any provision of this Agreement is found to be overly broad, unreasonable, or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, the court is authorized to modify (“blue pencil”) that provision to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable under applicable law (Arizona), or to void the specific provision only (California), while the remainder of the Agreement continues in full force and effect.

19Entire Agreement, Amendments, and Notices

19.1 Entire agreement. This Agreement, together with Appendices A, B, and C, constitutes the entire understanding of the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, representations, or understandings.

19.2 Amendments. Any modification must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

19.3 Notices. Notices under this Agreement are effective when delivered in person, by mail to the addresses above, or by email or text message (SMS) to the contact information provided by each Party.

19.4 Assignment. The Subcontractor may not assign this Agreement or subcontract an assigned job to another company without the Company’s prior written consent.

Appendix B

Scope of Services and Performance Standards

This Appendix B is part of the Independent Subcontractor Agreement between Simply Reflections LLC and the Subcontractor named in that Agreement. It describes each service the Company may assign to the Subcontractor and the standard to which that service must be performed. The Company’s Standard Operating Procedures supplement these standards and may be updated from time to time.

B1General Standards for All Services

B1.1 Assignment. The Company assigns work job by job. The Subcontractor may decline a job before it is scheduled, but once accepted must complete it as scoped on the job ticket, on the scheduled date and time, unless the Company agrees otherwise in writing.

B1.2 Scope discipline. Perform exactly what the job ticket scopes. Additional work a customer requests must be quoted and approved through the Company before it is performed. The Subcontractor does not set or change pricing.

B1.3 Workmanship. All work is performed in a professional, workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards and the Company’s Standard Operating Procedures (“SOP”), as updated from time to time, using tools, products, and methods appropriate to the surface or system being serviced. Where the SOP is more specific than this Appendix, the SOP controls the method of work.

B1.4 Photos and records. Take before-and-after photos of every job as the SOP requires and submit them with the daily job report under Section 10. A job without required photos is not complete.

B1.5 Safety. Follow Section 6 and the SOP safety rules at all times: inspect ladders and equipment before every use, never overreach, keep clear of power lines, wear required personal protective equipment, and stop work and report any unsafe condition to the Company.

B1.6 Customer property. Protect landscaping, fixtures, vehicles, furnishings, and finishes in and around the work area, and report any damage to the Company the same day in accordance with Section 9.

B1.7 Final walk-through. Complete the SOP quality-control checklist and a final walk-through with the customer (when present) before leaving every job.

B2Window Cleaning

B2.1 Service levels. Standard (Exterior): exterior glass, exterior frames, screens, bug and sun screens, and exposed sills — spotless, with no streaks, smudges, dirt, dust, or water spots. Standard (Interior): interior glass, exposed interior sills, and screens; interior tracks and frames are not included. Luxury (Full Service): all Standard items plus interior frames, vacuumed and cleaned tracks, detailed sills, debris removed, and windows lifted as needed.

B2.2 Method. Follow the SOP job sequence: meet the customer, remove and stack screens neatly, complete all interior cleaning, clean the screens, move outside, complete the exterior cleaning, and reinstall the screens. Begin inside unless the customer or the Company instructs otherwise. Use squeegee and water-fed-pole methods as the SOP directs and only Company-approved cleaning solutions.

B2.3 Finish. Glass free of streaks, drips, and residue; frames and sills wiped; screens clean and reinstalled correctly and securely; no water left on floors, sills, or furnishings.

B3Power Washing

B3.1 Surfaces. Driveways, sidewalks, patios, pool decks, exterior walls, fences, and other hard surfaces as scoped on the job ticket.

B3.2 Method. Select the pressure, nozzle, and standoff distance appropriate to each surface — lower pressure and a wider fan for stucco, wood, painted surfaces, and roofing — and never etch concrete, strip paint, or force water behind siding or into fixtures. Pre-wet and protect plants, outlets, light fixtures, and vehicles; pre-treat stains as the SOP directs; rinse thoroughly and direct runoff away from doors, windows, and storm drains where local rules require.

B3.3 Finish. Uniform, streak-free results with no striping, no surface damage, and no debris or standing wastewater left on the property.

B4Solar Panel Cleaning

B4.1 Method. Clean panels with purified or deionized water and soft brushes or microfiber only. No harsh chemicals, abrasives, or high pressure. Clean panels when they are cool (early morning or evening) to avoid thermal shock and spotting. Never step on panels and never disturb wiring, connectors, or mounting hardware.

B4.2 Photos required. Before-and-after photos are required for every solar panel job. A solar job without them is not complete.

B4.3 Reporting. Report cracked glass, loose hardware, damaged wiring, or nesting and pest activity to the Company the same day. Do not attempt repairs.

B5Bird Guard Installation

B5.1 Scope. Install bird and critter guard mesh around the perimeter of rooftop solar arrays to keep birds and animals from nesting beneath the panels.

B5.2 Method. Wearing required protective equipment, clear nests, droppings, and debris from beneath the array before installing. Measure the array perimeter, cut and fit mesh to size, and secure it with manufacturer-specified, non-penetrating clips. Do not drill into panels, racking, or the roof, and do not disconnect or reroute wiring, without the Company’s written authorization.

B5.3 Finish. Continuous, gap-free coverage with clean, straight cuts; no loose mesh, sharp edges, or debris left on the roof or ground; before-and-after photos submitted.

B6Garbage Can Cleaning

B6.1 Scope. Pressure-wash, sanitize, and deodorize residential and commercial waste and recycling bins as scoped.

B6.2 Method. Bag any remaining debris as the customer directs; clean the interior and exterior of each bin including lids and wheels; apply Company-approved sanitizer and deodorizer; and capture and dispose of wastewater lawfully — never into storm drains.

B6.3 Finish. Bins visibly clean, odor-free, and drained, returned to their storage location with lids closed.

B7Junk Removal and Hauling

B7.1 Scope. Load, remove, and haul away the items identified on the job ticket.

B7.2 Method. Confirm the item list with the customer before loading; protect floors, walls, and doorways; secure the load for transport; and dispose of items only at licensed disposal, recycling, or donation facilities, providing disposal receipts when the Company requests them. Do not accept hazardous materials — paint, chemicals, fuel, asbestos, medical waste, and the like — unless the Company has expressly authorized and priced them.

B7.3 Finish. Work area swept and clear, with nothing left curbside unless the customer and the Company have agreed.

B8Window and Patio Screens

B8.1 When it applies. Screen measurement, ordering, and installation are assigned only to subcontractors the Company designates for screen work and are governed by Appendix C.

B9Recurring Service, Reviews, and Upsells

B9.1 Program. Present the Company’s recurring service program at every eligible job as the SOP describes, invite customers to leave a review through the Company’s link, and offer only Company-approved add-on services at Company pricing. Never solicit tips, side work, or direct business.

Appendix C

Screen Measurement and Installation Schedule

This Appendix C is part of the Independent Subcontractor Agreement between Simply Reflections LLC and the Subcontractor named in that Agreement. It applies only when the Company assigns the Subcontractor screen measurement, ordering, or installation work.

C1Application

C1.1 When this Appendix applies. This Appendix C applies only when the Company assigns the Subcontractor to measure, order, or install window, patio, or door screens. When it applies, this Appendix controls over the general provisions of this Agreement as to its subject matter; all other terms of the Agreement remain in full force.

C2Engagement and Scope

C2.1 Scope. The Company engages the Subcontractor as an independent subcontractor to measure and install window, patio, and door screens on residential and, where assigned, commercial properties in Arizona and/or California. The Subcontractor performs all screen work on behalf of Simply Reflections and, if asked by any customer or third party, shall state that it is working on behalf of Simply Reflections. The Subcontractor shall not solicit or accept screen work from the Company’s leads or customers except through the Company.

C3Company Responsibilities

C3.1 Leads, pricing, and materials. Simply Reflections supplies all leads, determines all customer pricing, handles all orders, invoicing, and collection, provides the screen materials, and notifies the Subcontractor when materials are ready to install.

C4Subcontractor Responsibilities

C4.1 Measure, confirm, install. The Subcontractor takes accurate measurements and confirms them with the Company before any order is placed, installs in a professional, workmanlike manner, and supplies its own labor, tools, transportation, insurance, and any required licenses, including an Arizona ROC license where the work requires one.

C5Measurement Accuracy

C5.1 Subcontractor’s responsibility. The Subcontractor is solely responsible for measurement accuracy. If a screen must be re-ordered, re-fabricated, or remade, or any additional cost is incurred, because of a measurement error attributable to the Subcontractor, the Subcontractor shall pay all such costs, including the full replacement-screen cost, shipping, and restocking or re-fabrication charges. The Company may deduct these amounts from sums owed to the Subcontractor under Section 10. This does not apply to costs caused by the Company’s written specification error, manufacturer defects, or customer-directed changes made after confirmation.

C6Non-Refundable Materials and Payment Risk

C6.1 Manufacturer prepayment. The Subcontractor acknowledges that the manufacturer requires full payment up front, which is non-refundable once ordered, and that Simply Reflections fronts that payment and carries the funding risk. Where a re-order results from the Subcontractor’s measurement error, the Subcontractor is responsible for the re-order cost, including the manufacturer’s non-refundable charge, consistent with C5.

C7Lead Times

C7.1 Fabrication time. The Subcontractor acknowledges that screens may take approximately forty-five to ninety (45–90) days to be fabricated and returned before installation, per the manufacturer. Neither Party is liable for manufacturer or shipping delays outside its control.

C8Compensation for Screen Work

C8.1 Rate and timing. The Company pays the Subcontractor the per-screen, per-opening, or per-job rate agreed in writing for each job before work begins, within the payment window in Section 10, less any deductions under C5 and C6.
